# Settings and Profile as two pages (v71)

- **Bottom bar:** Games · Rankings · **Home** · Community · **Settings** (was Me). The profile opens from the picture in the header.
- **No shared tab row:** Profile and Settings each have their own title; the "My profile | Settings" tabs are gone.
- **Settings:** the three groups (Group, App, Notifications and data) are grouped lists of icon rows (`_navRow`, `.nav-rows`) instead of one card per item; sign out still asks first.
- **Profile:** hero (photo, name, email, permissions), three tiles (rating, games, win rate), badges, then story, personal info, gear, payment QR (all existing cards).
No data or rules change. **Version:** v71.
