How the estimate works

Session lengthyou set itThe booked time (3 h here). Warm-up 10 min is counted at the start.
Game lengthfrom live scoreLive score times every game, so the minutes are real, not guessed. Close games run longer (here 19 to 22 min against 10 to 11 for the easy ones). A game typed in by hand without minutes is left out of the estimate.
On courtfrom live scoreThe sum of your games' minutes. Rest and waiting is the session minus warm-up minus on court.
CaloriescalculatedMET x weight x hours. Badminton social play is about 5.5 MET, warm-up 4, resting about 2 (activity compendium values, to be re-checked before shipping).
Heart rate zonespersonalZ1 to Z5 are 50, 60, 70, 80 and 90% of your heart rate reserve above your resting rate (Karvonen). Max heart rate is the one you enter, else 208 minus 0.7 x age. Nobody shares the same zones unless their age and resting rate match.
Heart rate shapeillustrationCalculated as a share of your own heart rate reserve (max minus resting), so every player gets their own bpm. Warm-up ramps up, each game settles near 60% of reserve, rest drops toward 30%. A close game adds up to 10% of reserve and a late peak, a blowout lowers it. With a watch connected, real readings replace this curve.
kcal = MET x kg x hours · example: Ton: 5.5 x 70 kg x 2.42 h = 931 (on court, 145 min) + 4 x 70 x 0.17 h = 47 + 2 x 70 x 0.42 h = 59 = 1035, 825 above resting. Noi (62 kg) gets 917 / 731 from the same night.

Suggested fields and plan

Weight, heightweight neededPrivate page, stored in your own private profile record like your date of birth. Used only for calories.
Birth yearreuseAlready collected. Gives age and the default max heart rate.
Resting and max heart rateoptionalMakes the zones personal. Auto values are shown until you edit them.
Injuries or notesoptionalFree text only you see, for example "left knee". Suggested, not required for any number.
Weekly goaloptionalSessions or minutes per week, with a small progress ring on Home.
Share with groupoffOne switch that shares only totals (sessions, minutes, kcal), never weight, height or heart rate.
  1. Add the private Health details page to Profile and store it with the private profile data.
  2. Add an estimate card to each session result: duration, on court, calories, heart rate curve marked as an estimate.
  3. Let live score minutes and final scores feed the estimate, so close games show higher heart rate.
  4. Show a Health tab on Profile with period totals and trend, using the same All, month, 3m, 6m, 1y filters.
  5. Later, with the native app and a watch, replace the estimate with real readings (see the health tracking plan).

This is a wellness estimate for fun and motivation. It is not medical advice, and the app will say so on the card.
